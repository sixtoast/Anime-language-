## Findings

1. The specification's fixed-arity and unique-parse principles are documented, but source statements alone do not prove implementation conformance.
2. The current registry says FRAJA replaced FRATA; T-002 still needs execution against the parser to confirm argument order and alias handling.
3. FRASHA remains a known mismatch in the Phase 6H evaluator. Phase 6K tests only a reference negation contract, not the missing full runtime.
4. Human-listener, construction feasibility, conservation, and Logos accounting tests cannot be substituted with lexical CSV checks.
