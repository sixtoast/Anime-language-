# Phase 7F: Verb frames

Status: proposal, not canon.

Lexical glosses do not define argument roles. For each proposed verb, record its semantic roles, required and optional arguments, source provenance, register, examples, counterexamples and ambiguity tests. Leave unsupported surface forms unresolved.

Priority: distinguish giver, theme and recipient for GIVE; source, destination and path for GO/COME; experiencer and theme for SEE/HEAR; speaker, content and recipient for SAY; teacher, learner and content for TEACH; ability, permission and possibility for CAN; desire and necessity for WANT/NEED.

Do not infer canonical SVO from pilot sentences. Do not extend the proposed gloss of desh (in/at) to to/from without evidence. Do not invent role markers. Record ambiguous role assignments rather than guessing.
