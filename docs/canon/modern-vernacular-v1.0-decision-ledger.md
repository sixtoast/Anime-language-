# Modern Vernacular v1.0 — Decision Ledger and Provisional Backlog Closure

**Status:** Binding decision index for Modern Vernacular v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Companion specification:** [Arcane Modern Vernacular v1.0 — Locked Core Grammar](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.0-locked-core.md)

## 1. Authority and purpose

This ledger prevents the project from cycling through the same provisional questions. For the bounded v1.0 core, the companion locked grammar is authoritative. Older research records remain available for provenance, but their open proposals are superseded where they conflict with the locked specification.

A decision may be reopened only when at least one of the following exists:
1. primary-source evidence contradicts it;
2. a reproducible teaching/parser test fails;
3. an internal contradiction is demonstrated;
4. the language owner explicitly requests a versioned redesign.

Preference alone is not enough to silently change a locked rule. Changes require an amendment and regression fixtures.

## 3. What is not locked yet—and why

The project must not pretend that the bounded v1.0 core solves the whole language. The following are open work packages, not invitations to repeat the same architecture discussion indefinitely.

| Package | Open question | Next required deliverable | Closure criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP-01 Verb frames | What arguments do verbs other than wish license? | One consolidated frame table grounded in sources and contrastive examples | Each selected verb has typed roles, optionality, and rejection cases; unsupported frames stay blocked |
| VP-02 Participant marking | How are roles disambiguated outside the locked wish clause? | One decision across word order, role particles and adpositions | One coherent rule set plus collision/ambiguity fixtures; no new markers without lexicon audit |
| VP-03 Adpositions | What do desh, ding, push and buk attach to? | A single attachment/meaning contract | Distinguishes argument vs adjunct, attachment site and scope; no contradictory teaching examples |
| VP-04 Aspect | How do don and kok interact with verbs and negation? | One aspect system proposal or evidence recovery | States event phase, placement, scope and forbidden combinations |

| VP-06 Temporal grammar | How are time, duration, deadlines and ordering expressed? | One temporal anchor/unit/frame specification | Distinguishes point, interval, duration, endpoint and prediction with test pairs |

## 4. Disposition rules for provisional records

- A research record may remain labelled provisional when it documents a genuinely open work package.
- When a rule is locked, all older records that proposed alternatives must carry a supersession notice pointing to the authoritative grammar.
- A repeated example is not evidence unless an independent review or test is recorded.
- No file may claim “validated”, “passed”, “tested” or “canonical” without the corresponding evidence.
- Do not add more planning documents for a package once its closure criterion can be met. Implement the decision, patch the learner grammar and run the available checks.
- Where the available tooling cannot execute a speaker or parser test, label it unrun and do not use it to block a design decision forever. A bounded modern rule can be locked by owner-authorised design while empirical validation remains a separate status.

## 5. Required completion loop

For each package, follow this sequence:

1. Inspect the source and existing tests once.
2. Choose: recover evidence, make an explicit modern design decision, or keep the construction blocked.
3. Write one authoritative rule set.
4. Add positive, contrast, negative and blocked examples.
5. Patch the relevant lexicon/learner material and mark old proposals superseded.
6. Run the checks actually available; record those not available.
7. Close the package or record the single specific dependency preventing closure.

Do not create another phase whose only result is a slightly revised comparison table.

## 7. Version 1.1 extension

The separate [Modern Vernacular v1.1 Core Verb Expansion](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.1-core-verb-expansion.md) locks six narrow frames: nak, zir, shus, mos, mol and tut. It extends nava, pun, shuk and zosh to this closed list only.

This does not alter the v1.0 baseline. It closes part of VP-01 (verb frames). Motion roles for ful/zem; transfer/source roles for bul/lil; teaching roles for pup; task complements for las; endpoints for shem; modality for fam; event complements for kong/tek; and unrestricted propositional complements remain open or blocked.

**Current status:** v1.0 is a compatibility baseline; v1.1 adds these six verb frames. Neither claims independent speaker validation or parser execution.

## 8. Version 1.2 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.2 Transfer and Recipients specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.2-transfer-and-recipients.md) locks a bounded transfer construction:
- bul with an explicit li item and ka recipient for “give”;
- bul with an explicit li item and du source for the recipient-centred “receive” frame;
- lil with an explicit li item and du source for “take”.

The particles ka, du and li are modern design coinages restricted to these frames. They are not yet general case markers or unrestricted adpositions. Questions for recipient/source roles remain blocked.

This closes one bounded part of transfer-role design, not the full transfer/possession system. Ownership, possession, permission, consent, successful delivery and general recipient-question syntax remain open.

## 9. Version 1.3 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.3 Motion and Routes specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.3-motion-and-routes.md) locks bounded motion clauses for ful (go/travel) and zem (come), with tava marking a goal, dava a source and yara a route. These are modern design coinages restricted to those motion frames. They do not reinterpret Formal JO/JUR/JA or the transfer particles ka/du/li.

Questions for destination/source/route, multiple same-role phrases, tense/aspect, manner and general spatial adpositions remain blocked. This closes a bounded motion construction, not the full spatial grammar.

## 10. Version 1.4 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.4 Modality and Intention specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.4-modality-and-intention.md) locks a bounded modal construction: subject + fam/kong/tek + a licensed action frame, with the modal subject controlling the action subject. Fam means ability/capacity, kong means desire, and tek means practical or goal-dependent need; permission, moral/legal obligation and guaranteed action are not implied. Kong and tek may also take bounded noun-phrase complements, while fam may not. Positive modal constructions can take pun; nava with modals is deliberately blocked until scope is specified. Existing action frames and their role markers remain binding.

This closes a bounded part of the modality work package. Negative modality, permission, obligation, different-subject complements, unrestricted propositional embedding, tense/aspect and other mood meanings remain open or blocked. The modern design decision does not establish historical attestation; newly recovered old documents must be examined as source evidence and reconciled through a transparent versioned amendment if they materially contradict this specification.

## 11. Version 1.5 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.5 Permission specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.5-permission.md) introduces yemi as a modern permission-status modal. It is distinct from fam (ability), kong (desire) and tek (practical need). It uses the subject-controlled modal-action pattern, allows positive polar questions with pun, and preserves the action verb's existing frame. It does not identify a grantor, entail action or consent, or license negation, prohibition, modal stacking or a separate grantor argument. The inspected v5 draft lexicon had no exact yemi or permission/allow/permit entry for this construction; this is a bounded collision check, not proof that no uninspected source contains the form.

This closes a bounded permission-status construction. Explicit granting, prohibition, permission negation and consent remain open or blocked. Historical Arcane remains unchanged; recovered source material should be compared transparently if Arcana Mega Pack2 becomes accessible.

## 12. Version 1.6 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.6 Modal Negation and Prohibition specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.6-modal-negation-and-prohibition.md) defines scope in modal constructions: nava before the modal negates the modal proposition; nava immediately before the action verb negates the action within modal scope. It introduces zami as a modern coinage for explicit prohibition, distinct from inability (fam) and absence of permission (nava yemi). Positive and negative modal/prohibition propositions may take pun; the question particle does not alter scope. Modal stacking, negated zami and zami+yemi combinations remain blocked. The v5 lexicon check found no exact zami entry; this is not proof of absence from uninspected historical sources.

This closes the bounded modal-negation and prohibition construction at specification level. Parser and independent-speaker validation are not claimed. Historical and Formal Arcane remain unchanged; if Arcana Mega Pack2 becomes accessible, relevant evidence should be recorded and compared before any amendment.

## 13. Version 1.7 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.7 Directives, Requests, Advice and Instructions specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.7-directives-and-requests.md) introduces four modern speech-act markers: dema (direct command), sori (request), mira (advice/recommendation) and toku (procedural instruction). The marker begins the utterance and scopes over a licensed action frame; the addressee is the understood actor. Underlying verb frames and role particles remain binding. Directive negation, directive questions, modal stacking and separate unexpressed actors remain blocked. The inspected v5 draft lexicon contained vanu as a Formal velocity entry, so it was deliberately not reused as an advice marker. The inspected v5 draft lexicon had no exact entries for dema, sori, mira or toku; this is a bounded collision check, not proof of absence from uninspected historical sources.

This closes a bounded speech-act layer, not the full imperative, politeness, authority, procedure sequencing or command-negation systems. Parser and independent-speaker validation are not claimed. Historical and Formal Arcane remain unchanged.

## 14. Version 1.8 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.8 Clause Coordination and Causal Links specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.8-clause-linking.md) adds lena (and, non-causal coordination), savi (because, following clause gives the reason for the preceding clause), and doru (therefore/as a result, following clause is the consequence of the preceding clause). Each side must be an independently licensed complete clause; participants remain clause-local. The linkers mark the speaker's asserted relation but do not prove its objective truth. Directive coordination, nested linkers, ambiguous broad question scope, conditionals, temporal sequence and reported speech remain blocked. Exact-form checks found no entries for lena, savi or doru in the inspected v5 draft lexicon; this is not a claim about uninspected sources. No parser or independent-speaker validation is claimed.

## 15. Version 1.9 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v1.9 Conditions, Hypotheses and Consequences specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v1.9-conditionals-and-hypotheses.md) adds navi (IF), saku (THEN/conditional consequence), and ravi (SUPPOSE). The core conditional is navi CONDITION saku CONSEQUENCE; both clauses must be independently licensed and the condition is not thereby asserted as actual. ravi is restricted to a written supposition followed by an explicitly marked navi…saku conditional, with a semicolon used in teaching notation to make the boundary visible. Conditional, causal (savi) and inferential/consequence (doru) constructions remain distinct. Nested conditionals, directive conditionals, conditional questions, broad conditional negation scope and external coordination remain blocked. Exact-form checks found no entries for navi, saku or ravi in the inspected v5 draft lexicon; this is not a claim about uninspected historical sources. Parser and independent-speaker validation are not claimed.

## 16. Mega Pack2 source audit

The exact archive `docs/Arcana megapack2.zip` was found and indexed. See [Mega Pack2 Language Evidence Audit 01](../research/megapack2-language-evidence-audit-01.md), [archive manifest](../../sources/archive-reports/megapack2-report.md), and [searchable text index](../../sources/archive-reports/megapack2-text-index.md). The archive contains 280 non-directory members, 49 exact duplicate groups, 253 unique readable text documents and no read/extraction errors or size-limit skips in the implemented pass; PDF text was not extracted. The Phase 5 v5 lexicon records 115 Vernacular entries as PROPOSED. It confirms that many roots already used in Modern Vernacular v1.0–v1.5 (including teshi, fezo, wish, shuk, zosh, nak, zir, shus, mos, mol, tut, bul, lil, ful, zem, fam, kong and tek) are Phase 5 proposed lexicon entries rather than newly coined forms. Modern grammar decisions remain a later, bounded layer; source statuses are not rewritten. The same lexicon records don (already/done) and kok (ongoing) as Vernacular / PROPOSED / Phase 5. Formal temporal proposals such as boni, vote, fuwo, kole, shasu, wela, vitu, showa and leko are not thereby promoted into ordinary Vernacular grammar. The archive also confirms that ka is a Shared / CANON / v3.0-L axis token (-Z) and du is a Shared / CANON / v3.0-L digit (7), while the v1.2 transfer roles use those same strings in tightly restricted slots. The v1.2 specification now records this collision and requires future parsers to preserve the axis/digit readings outside the recipient/source slots. The archive's Phase 7W runtime is explicitly provisional; its captured 17-test result validates only that implementation snapshot, not canonical language grammar.

## 17. Version 2.0 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v2.0 Completion and Ongoing Aspect specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v2.0-completion-and-ongoing-aspect.md) uses the source-listed Phase 5 Vernacular proposals `don` (completed by a reference time) and `kok` (ongoing at a reference time). It locks their placement before a bounded set of action verbs, defines the readings of `nava don` (not completed by reference time) and `nava kok` (not in progress at reference time), and blocks aspect stacking, modal/aspect scope, directive/aspect combinations and negative polar aspect questions. This is aspect, not a general past/present/future tense system; temporal adverbs, duration, deadline, repetition and event sequence remain unresolved. Parser and independent-speaker validation are not claimed.

## 19. Version 2.1 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v2.1 Temporal Anchor and Event Order specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v2.1-temporal-anchor-and-event-order.md) introduces `nemi` (explicit current-time anchor), `pavo` (first completed event precedes the second), and `sula` (first completed event follows the second). These are modern design coinages after an exact-form check against the inspected Mega Pack2 v5 draft lexicon. `nemi` is restricted to aspect-marked clauses using `don` or `kok`; `pavo` and `sula` connect exactly two independently licensed completed-event clauses using `don`. Ongoing-event ordering, event chains, clock time, duration, deadlines, repetition and prediction remain blocked. Temporal order is distinct from causation and conditionality. Formal Phase 4 `boni`, `vote` and `fuwo` remain Formal / PROPOSED; the Phase 5 generator's shared mapping is recorded as a cross-register inconsistency, not a promotion into Vernacular canon. Sixteen specification fixtures are documented but have not been parser-executed or independently speaker-tested.


## 20. Version 2.2 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v2.2 Qualitative Event Duration specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v2.2-qualitative-duration.md) introduces `melo` (briefly; short duration) and `runi` (for a long time; long duration) as modern design coinages after an exact-form check against the inspected Mega Pack2 v5 draft lexicon. They occur clause-finally only with affirmative `don` clauses whose eventive verb is licensed by v2.0. Duration with `kok`, negative completion, modal clauses, numeric time quantities, delay, waiting, deadlines, endpoints and repetition remain outside scope. Duration is distinguished from speed, frequency, completion, and event order. Sixteen specification fixtures are documented but have not been parser-executed or independently speaker-tested.


## 21. Version 2.3 extension

The [Modern Vernacular v2.3 Waiting and Until specification](arcane-modern-vernacular-v2.3-waiting-and-until.md) introduces `seno` (wait; remain in a waiting state) and `kavi` (until) as modern design coinages after an exact-form check against the inspected Mega Pack2 v5 draft lexicon. Aspect is explicitly extended to `seno`; the only endpoint construction currently licensed is `SUBJECT kok seno kavi ENDPOINT-SUBJECT don zem`. The endpoint event is asserted, and the construction does not entail causation, meeting, or communication. The Formal proposals `kole` (until) and `shasu` (delay/offset before start) remain Formal / PROPOSED. Delay is not yet expressed because a baseline and offset representation are not defined. Eighteen design fixtures are documented but have not been parser-executed or independently speaker-tested.
