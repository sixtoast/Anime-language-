# Decision Records

Record meaningful language-design and implementation decisions here.

Each decision record should include:

- A stable identifier and concise title.
- Status: proposed, accepted, rejected, superseded, or unresolved.
- The question being decided and the options considered.
- Evidence and source links, including conflicting evidence.
- The rationale and known consequences for grammar, vocabulary, magic-system rules, code, and tests.
- The date and the decision's relationship to earlier decisions, where known.

Never erase the history of a superseded decision. Link to its replacement and explain what changed.
